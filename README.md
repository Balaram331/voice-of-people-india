# AITTS - All India The Truth Speaker 🇮🇳

## Why AITTS Was Built

In today's digital world, many people hesitate to share their real problems and opinions because most social platforms require creating accounts, revealing personal information, and exposing identities publicly.

AITTS was created to solve this problem.

The platform allows anyone to raise their voice without creating an account. Users can share their thoughts, concerns, and social issues anonymously, ensuring privacy while encouraging honest expression.

Our goal is simple:

**Give every citizen a voice, without fear and without barriers.**

---

## The Problem

Most existing social platforms:

- Require account creation.
- Collect personal user data.
- Discourage people from sharing sensitive issues.
- Focus more on entertainment than meaningful public discussions.

As a result, many important voices remain unheard.

---

## Our Solution

AITTS provides:

- Anonymous issue posting.
- No account creation required.
- Minimal user data collection.
- Community-driven support system.
- A platform where people can freely express their thoughts.

---

## Additional Features

### AI Assistant

An AI chatbot has been integrated to guide users throughout the website, answer common questions, and improve the overall user experience.

### Game Hub

Interactive games were added to improve user retention and engagement, encouraging visitors to spend more time on the platform and return regularly.

### Trending Issues

Issues that receive more community support automatically become trending, increasing their visibility.
