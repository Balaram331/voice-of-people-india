🔥 Trending Issue

Overcrowded Government Hospitals and Lack of Medical Staff: Government hospitals in many parts of India, especially in states like Bihar, are heavily overcrowded and understaffed. Patients often have to wait for hours or even days to receive basic treatment. The shortage of doctors, nurses, and proper medical equipment creates serious challenges, particularly for low-income families who depend entirely on public healthcare. This leads to delayed treatment, poor patient care, and increased health risks.

📢 How This Platform Works

To share your issue, click on the “Submit Your Voice” button on the homepage and fill out the form with your name, state, city, and your problem. Once you submit it, we take a quick look and then publish it in the Recent Voices section so others can see it. Anyone can raise their voice here — there are no complicated steps or barriers. If people relate to your issue, they can show support by liking ❤️ or supporting ⭐ it. Issues that receive more support automatically move to the Trending section so that more people can see them. You can also read other people’s issues and support them if you feel the same.

Common Issues

Unemployment

Millions of young people struggle to find jobs despite having education and skills. Lack of opportunities, low wages, and job insecurity are major concerns affecting the future of youth.

Healthcare

Access to affordable and quality healthcare remains a challenge. Many people face issues like expensive treatments, lack of hospitals, and poor medical facilities in rural areas.

Education

Quality education is still not accessible to everyone. Many students face problems like outdated systems, lack of resources, and inequality between urban and rural education.

Infrastructure

Basic infrastructure like roads, transport, electricity, and sanitation is still underdeveloped in many areas, affecting daily life and economic growth.

Our Mission

About AITTS

AITTS stands for All India The Truth Speaker — a vision-driven initiative built to bring real voices of people into the spotlight.

In a country where millions of people face daily struggles, many voices remain unheard. AITTS was created to change that by giving every individual a space to speak openly about real issues without fear.

It is not just a name — it represents a movement where truth matters, voices unite, and real problems are highlighted for awareness and change.

Through this platform, AITTS aims to connect people, amplify their concerns, and turn individual voices into a powerful collective impact.
